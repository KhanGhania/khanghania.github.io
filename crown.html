Renal
Warriorsixteen years strong

I was diagnosed with chronic kidney disease as a high-school student in 2010. Since then: dialysis, a transplant, dialysis again, and a first-class degree, an agency and a leadership role along the way. Renal Warrior is where I turned that fight into a platform for patients, caregivers and families.

Forged in fire
The journey

Every setback, followed by a rise.

30 May 2010

Diagnosed with CKD

Still in high school. It began with fatigue, breathlessness and swelling, which doctors first suspected was a heart problem.

10 June 2010

First dialysis

Dangerously high potassium meant dialysis could not wait. The start of sessions three times a week, alongside school.

2016

Kidney transplant

A new chapter, and later an honest one about the depression and panic attacks that can follow, which I now write about openly.

2020 – 2023

First in class at Jamia Hamdard

Graduated BCA with 92%, while leading The Mental Health Club and designing for clients through Flaxen Designs.

2023

Back on dialysis

That same year I designed remotely for Leyton Contracting in Riyadh and completed a graphic design internship at Simpana Technologies.

September 2024

Renal Warrior launches

I designed, built and wrote renalwarrior.in from scratch: brand, crowned-lion logo, web design and every article.

March 2025

World Kidney Day speaker

Invited as a guest speaker at Jamia Hamdard to share my experience of living with CKD.

October 2026

Senior Marketing & Sales Manager

Promoted from intern within one month at RBP Finivis. The fire never ended the story. It started the next chapter.

The mark

A lion, half solid, half starlight.

I designed the Renal Warrior logo as a crowned lion drawn in two ways at once: a sculpted face in rose-gold facets, and a body mapped as a constellation of points and lines. It is the same lion that forms out of light on the home page of this site.
