02 The month that earned the promotion

Mego Forex: fixing the funnel before buying reach

Consumer forex brand of an RBI-licensed Full Fledged Money Changer. Facebook, Instagram and WhatsApp, with Zoho CRM.

AuditStrategyPaid testingCRMCompliance

The brand could buy attention cheaply, but reach wasn't turning into conversations. My diagnosis: a conversion problem dressed up as a content problem. Spending more would only have bought more clicks into the same dead end.

  1. DiagnoseA 15-section channel audit across two reporting windows, a full grid review and a conversion-funnel teardown.
  2. Fix the bottom of the funnelClick-to-WhatsApp routing, a named inbox owner with a two-hour target, and ten approved saved replies.
  3. Make every lead traceableLead Source mandatory in Zoho CRM and tracked links per channel, so marketing's contribution can be proven.
  4. Govern the claimsA one-line approval rule for any creative with a number, and approved licence wording.
  5. Rebuild the content engineEight content buckets, a reels-led mix, Hindi/English testing and a four-week calendar.
  6. Test, then scaleA small paid layer run in four A/B rounds to find the brand's first reliable cost per conversation.

Two company websites, re-planned for search

I audited the RBP Finivis and Royal Bank Pacific websites and handed each build team everything they need: structure, page-by-page content, keywords and technical SEO. The developers build; I decide what goes where, and why.

Website auditInformation architectureSEOCopywritingDeveloper handover

04 Programme design · Oct 2026

Mego Internship Program, reimagined

A 23-slide proposal and 7-page plan that turns a 45-day internship into a training-to-career pathway, growing the group's sales and reach while giving students something real in return.

Programme designSales trainingHiring pipelineAdvocacyZoho CRM
  1. Stage 1 · Sales training, 45 daysEvery selected trainee: live weekly training, commission from day one and a tiered certificate.
  2. Stage 2 · Sales internship, 3 monthsThe top performers of each batch earn a real internship, an experience letter and a performance stipend.
  3. Stage 3 · CareerA full-time sales role or a referral-partner path, with alumni training future batches.
  • Selection that cheating can't beat: an open-book module, a 90-second video pitch and a 10-day probation with real leads.
  • Certificates that mean something: Completion, Merit and Distinction tiers, each verifiable by QR code.
  • A Zoho leaderboard where closing a sale earns far more than a like, so incentives point at revenue.
  • Advocacy that looks real: own-caption reposts and genuine comments instead of copy-paste engagement that platforms penalise.
  • Two doors: a selective internship for students and an open partner programme for everyone else.
  • Guardrails: no cash or ID documents handled by trainees, data kept in the CRM, approved claims only.
